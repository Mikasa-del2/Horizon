## [Non-epitaxial perovskite polariton laser diode under DC](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10824-w) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Researchers demonstrated the first non-epitaxial perovskite polariton laser diode that operates under a constant direct current at 8 K, using a solution-grown CsPbBr3 microplate integrated with single-walled carbon nanotube electrodes and an optical microcavity. This breakthrough overcomes a major hurdle in polariton laser technology—achieving electrical injection without epitaxial growth—paving the way for low-cost, energy-efficient laser diodes that can be fabricated via solution processing, with broad implications for optoelectronics and nanophotonics. The device operates in the strong coupling regime and exhibits polariton lasing under a direct current of 65 μA at 8 K. The use of chemically inert single-walled carbon nanotube electrodes enables stable electrical injection into the perovskite microplate.

rss · Nature - Latest Research · Jul 22, 00:00

**Background**: Polariton lasers exploit exciton-polaritons—hybrid light-matter quasiparticles—to achieve low-threshold lasing. Traditional polariton lasers require epitaxially grown semiconductor structures, which are costly and complex. Halide perovskites have emerged as promising solution-processable materials for optoelectronics, but electrically pumped perovskite lasers have remained elusive until now.

## [Insulator-free topological photonic multi-lane highways](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10817-9) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Researchers have demonstrated a novel insulator-free topological photonic waveguide that uses four inequivalent photonic valley half-semimetals arranged in a parallel, cyclic configuration to achieve robust, multi-lane unidirectional light transport with 100% spatial efficiency. This breakthrough could significantly advance photonic integrated circuits by enabling more compact and efficient on-chip optical interconnects, potentially leading to faster and more energy-efficient data communication. The waveguide architecture is insulator-free, meaning it does not require a photonic bandgap material, and achieves perfect spatial efficiency by stacking four valley half-semimetals very close together. The design supports multiple lanes of unidirectional light transport that are robust against defects.

rss · Nature - Latest Research · Jul 22, 00:00

**Background**: Topological photonics uses concepts from topology to protect light propagation against scattering and losses. Traditional topological waveguides often require an insulator (bandgap) material to confine light, which limits spatial efficiency. Valley half-semimetals are a class of materials where the conduction and valence bands touch at discrete points, enabling unique topological properties.

## [Jupiter-mass exomoon candidate detected via radial velocity](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10751-w) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Radial velocity measurements have provided evidence for a Jupiter-mass exosatellite orbiting the brown dwarf CD-35 2722 B, marking a promising detection of an exomoon-like object published in Nature on July 22, 2026. This detection represents a significant milestone in exomoon research, as no exomoons have been confirmed to date, and it demonstrates the radial velocity method's potential for discovering such elusive objects, which could impact our understanding of planetary system formation and habitability. The exosatellite candidate has a Jupiter-like mass and orbits a brown dwarf, which itself is a substellar companion to the star CD-35 2722; the detection was made using radial velocity data, an indirect method that measures Doppler shifts in the host's spectrum.

rss · Nature - Latest Research · Jul 22, 00:00

**Background**: Exomoons, or exosatellites, are natural satellites orbiting exoplanets or other non-stellar bodies. They are extremely difficult to detect with current techniques, and no confirmed exomoon exists yet. The radial velocity method detects periodic shifts in a star's spectrum caused by an orbiting object's gravitational pull, and has been used to discover over 1,100 exoplanets. Brown dwarfs are substellar objects with masses between 13 and 80 Jupiter masses, too low for sustained hydrogen fusion but capable of deuterium fusion.

This discovery identifies TLS as a reservoir of potent anti-tumor T cells, offering new targets for cancer immunotherapy and potentially improving treatment outcomes for renal cell carcinoma patients. The study found that TLS-enriched tumors contain exhausted CD8+ T cells, including tumor-specific clonotypes with stem-like progenitor features and reduced terminal exhaustion, suggesting these cells may sustain long-term anti-tumor responses.

rss · Nature - Latest Research · Jul 22, 00:00

**Background**: Tertiary lymphoid structures (TLS) are ectopic lymphoid aggregates that form in non-lymphoid tissues during chronic inflammation or cancer. Stem-like CD8+ T cells are a subset of immune cells with self-renewal capacity and superior persistence, often associated with better responses to checkpoint blockade immunotherapy. Terminal exhaustion refers to a state of T cell dysfunction caused by persistent antigen exposure, characterized by loss of effector functions.

rss · Nature - Latest Research · Jul 22, 00:00

**Background**: Directed evolution is a protein engineering method that mimics natural selection by iteratively mutating a gene, selecting for desired traits, and amplifying the best variants. Traditionally, the starting point is a natural protein, which may limit the accessible sequence space. AI can now design synthetic starting points that are more evolvable, potentially leading to better outcomes.
