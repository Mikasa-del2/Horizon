## [Piezochiral Effect: Strain Controls Chirality](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10845-5) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Researchers have introduced the piezochiral effect, a new physical phenomenon where mechanical strain induces chirality in non-chiral crystals, published in Nature on July 29, 2026. This discovery expands the family of strain-responsive functionalities alongside piezoelectricity and piezomagnetism, with potential applications in photonics, spintronics, biosensing, and quantum information. Uniaxial strain rearranges atoms in crystals to create left- or right-handed structures, as demonstrated by monitoring optical activity. The effect is mathematically described and a symmetry classification of piezochiral materials has been established.

rss · Nature - Latest Research · Jul 29, 00:00

**Background**: Chirality refers to the property of an object being non-superimposable on its mirror image, like left and right hands. Piezoelectricity generates electric charge under mechanical stress, while piezomagnetism produces magnetization. The piezochiral effect adds a new dimension by enabling strain to control chirality directly.

## [Bacterial STAND NTPases sense core phage proteome](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10852-6) ⭐️ 9.0/10

A systematic study published in Nature reveals that prokaryotic STAND NTPases, relatives of animal immune receptors, act as diverse antiviral sensors that detect most core structural and replicative proteins of bacteriophages. This discovery significantly advances our understanding of bacterial innate immunity, revealing a broad antiviral sensing mechanism analogous to eukaryotic pattern recognition. It has high potential impact on microbiology, phage therapy, and synthetic biology. The study systematically analyzed prokaryotic STAND NTPases, showing they recognize conserved folds, active-site residues, and enzyme ligands of phage proteins, triggering tetramerization and activation of diverse effector domains like DNA endonucleases.

rss · Nature - Latest Research · Jul 29, 00:00

**Background**: STAND NTPases are a class of P-loop NTPases that include animal and plant immune receptors such as NLRs. Previous work showed that antiviral STAND (Avs) homologs in bacteria and archaea detect hallmark viral proteins, but the full scope of their sensing capabilities was unknown. This study provides a systematic analysis of the prokaryotic STAND NTPase family and its role in sensing the core phage proteome.

## [Mitochondrial metabolism drives SASP via epigenetic crosstalk](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10791-2) ⭐️ 9.0/10

A study published in Nature reveals that mitochondria-derived acetyl-CoA promotes histone acetylation and chromatin remodeling at inflammatory gene loci, driving the senescence-associated secretory phenotype (SASP). Inhibition of the mitochondrial citrate carrier SLC25A1 attenuates these effects, suggesting a new therapeutic target for delaying aging. This work establishes a direct mechanistic link between mitochondrial metabolism and epigenetic regulation of SASP, a key driver of age-related inflammation and functional decline. Targeting SLC25A1 offers a promising strategy to mitigate the detrimental effects of senescence and potentially extend healthspan. The study demonstrates that senescent cells upregulate mitochondrial acetyl-CoA production, which increases histone acetylation and chromatin accessibility specifically at SASP gene loci. Pharmacological or genetic inhibition of SLC25A1, the mitochondrial citrate carrier, reduces acetyl-CoA export to the cytosol and blunts SASP expression.

rss · Nature - Latest Research · Jul 29, 00:00

**Background**: Cellular senescence is a state of irreversible growth arrest accompanied by the secretion of pro-inflammatory factors, collectively termed the senescence-associated secretory phenotype (SASP). SASP contributes to chronic inflammation and tissue dysfunction during aging. Acetyl-CoA is a key metabolite that serves as a substrate for histone acetylation, a chromatin modification that promotes gene expression. SLC25A1 is a mitochondrial transporter that exports citrate (a precursor of acetyl-CoA) from mitochondria to the cytosol.

## [CO2 and bicycloalkane copolymerization yields recyclable polyesters](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10848-2) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Researchers report a simple organic catalyst that directly enables alternating copolymerization of CO2 with bicycloalkanes, producing high-performance polyesters that can be selectively depolymerized and recycled in a closed-loop lifecycle. This breakthrough addresses two major challenges: plastic waste and carbon utilization, by turning CO2 into valuable, recyclable materials. It could pave the way for sustainable plastics that are both high-performance and circular. The catalyst is an organic (metal-free) compound, and the bicycloalkane monomers are strained rings that enable efficient copolymerization with CO2. The resulting polyesters exhibit high thermal and mechanical performance and can be depolymerized back to monomers under mild conditions.

rss · Nature - Latest Research · Jul 29, 00:00

**Background**: Alternating copolymerization involves two monomers that insert alternately into the polymer chain, giving a regular structure. CO2 is an abundant, cheap, and renewable C1 feedstock, but its copolymerization with epoxides typically requires metal catalysts and yields polycarbonates, not polyesters. Bicycloalkanes are cyclic hydrocarbons with two fused or bridged rings; their ring strain provides the driving force for copolymerization with CO2 to form ester linkages.

This discovery provides a fundamental understanding of ion transport and phase transitions in lithium-ion battery electrodes, which could lead to improved battery design and performance. The study, published in Nature on July 29, 2026, shows that the dilute stages of graphite exhibit avalanche-like behavior during both deintercalation and intercalation, with local disorder playing a key role.

rss · Nature - Latest Research · Jul 29, 00:00

**Background**: In lithium-ion batteries, graphite is commonly used as the anode material, where lithium ions intercalate and deintercalate during charging and discharging. Phase transitions in graphite involve staging phenomena, and understanding their dynamics is crucial for battery efficiency and lifespan. Operando optical microscopy allows real-time, spatially resolved observation of these processes.

## [Nanopore 'chop and measure' sequences peptides at single-amino-acid resolution](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10881-1) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Researchers published a Nature paper describing a nanopore-based method that sequences peptides by progressively shortening the N-terminus one residue at a time using enzymatic digestion, enabling single-amino-acid resolution. This breakthrough could revolutionize proteomics by enabling direct, single-molecule peptide sequencing without the need for mass spectrometry, potentially accelerating protein identification and biomarker discovery. The method, termed 'chop and measure', uses an enzyme to sequentially cleave amino acids from the N-terminus, and the nanopore reads the remaining peptide after each cleavage to determine the identity of the removed residue.

rss · Nature - Latest Research · Jul 29, 00:00

**Background**: Nanopore sequencing is a single-molecule technique that measures changes in ionic current as molecules pass through a nanoscale pore. While widely used for DNA and RNA sequencing, peptide sequencing via nanopores has been challenging due to the lack of a natural motor protein and the similar size of amino acids. Previous approaches often required chemical modifications or threading tails to facilitate translocation.

## [Global centromere sequencing reveals rapid evolution](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10841-9) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Researchers have completely sequenced over 2,000 centromeres from diverse human populations, revealing their structures and showing that centromeres have elevated mutation rates, especially at the kinetochore site. This is the first comprehensive view of human centromere variation and evolution, providing insights into chromosome segregation, genomic stability, and evolutionary biology. The study validated mutation rates in a four-generation family of 28 members with 483 accurately assembled centromeres, showing the kinetochore region mutates twice as fast as flanking sequences.

rss · Nature - Latest Research · Jul 29, 00:00

**Background**: Centromeres are constricted regions of chromosomes that recruit the kinetochore, essential for chromosome segregation during cell division. They consist of repetitive alpha satellite DNA and are notoriously difficult to sequence due to their repetitive nature.

## [Kimi K3: Open-Weight Model Reaches Frontier with Novel Engineering](https://www.reddit.com/r/MachineLearning/comments/1vaysjf/how_kimi_k3_engineered_its_way_to_the_frontier_r/) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Moonshot released Kimi K3, an open-weight 2.8-trillion-parameter Mixture-of-Experts model that ranks fourth among 580 models on Artificial Analysis, behind only Claude Opus 5, Fable 5, and GPT-5.6 Sol. The model introduces three key innovations: Kimi Delta Attention, Quantile Balancing, and AgentENV. Kimi K3 demonstrates that open-weight models can compete with the best proprietary systems, potentially democratizing access to frontier AI. Its engineering innovations—especially the attention and load-balancing techniques—could influence future LLM designs across the industry. Kimi Delta Attention replaces the KV cache in 69 of 93 layers with a 128x128 matrix per head, reducing 1M-token context memory from 104.6 GiB to 27.2 GiB. Quantile Balancing keeps 896 experts per layer evenly loaded by computing bias directly from router score margins, avoiding the instability of fixed-step bias nudging used in DeepSeek-V3.

reddit · r/MachineLearning · /u/noninertialframe96 · Jul 30, 16:37

**Background**: Large language models often use Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architectures to scale parameters without proportionally increasing compute. However, MoE models face load-balancing challenges where some experts are underutilized. Additionally, traditional attention mechanisms require large KV caches for long contexts, limiting efficiency. Kimi K3 addresses both issues with novel techniques.
