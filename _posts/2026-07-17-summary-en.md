## [Brain Encodes Two Speech Streams Simultaneously, Study Finds](https://journals.plos.org/plosbiology/article?id=10.1371/journal.pbio.3003876) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A PLOS Biology study using EEG recordings from normal-hearing adults in an immersive multi-talker environment found that the brain can simultaneously encode two competing speech streams, challenging the long-held single-channel auditory bottleneck theory. This finding overturns a foundational assumption in cognitive science about limited auditory processing capacity, with potential implications for understanding attention, designing hearing aids, and developing brain-computer interfaces for speech decoding. Participants were cued to switch attention between two speech streams every 15–30 seconds, and EEG analysis revealed neural representations of both streams even when one was unattended. The study provides direct neural evidence against the idea that the brain processes only one speech stream at a time.

hackernews · giuliomagnifico · Jul 17, 05:51 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48943745)

**Background**: The auditory bottleneck theory, proposed by Broadbent in 1958, suggests that the human brain has a limited capacity for processing auditory information and can only attend to one stream at a time. This study used EEG to measure neural encoding of competing speech streams, providing a more direct test of this theory than behavioral experiments alone.

## [First True Sugar Molecule Detected in Interstellar Space](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-02263-4) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Astronomers have detected erythrulose, a four-carbon 'true sugar' molecule, in interstellar space for the first time. The discovery was reported in Nature on July 17, 2026. This finding provides key insights into prebiotic chemistry, showing that complex sugars can form under interstellar conditions. It has significant implications for understanding the origins of life and the chemical evolution of the universe. Erythrulose is a simple sugar found in raspberries and used in self-tanning lotions. Surprisingly, the four-carbon sugar appears to be at least eight times more abundant in space than analogous three-carbon sugars.

rss · Nature - Latest Research · Jul 17, 00:00

**Background**: The interstellar medium contains a rich variety of molecules, including many complex organic species considered prebiotic. Previously, only simpler sugars like glycolaldehyde (a two-carbon sugar) had been detected in space. This detection of a true sugar with four carbons represents a major step forward in astrochemistry.

## [Rocky Exoplanet LHS 1140b Found to Have an Atmosphere](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-02200-5) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Astronomers detected helium escaping from the atmosphere of LHS 1140b, a rocky super-Earth in the habitable zone of its star, marking the first confirmed atmosphere on a rocky exoplanet in that zone. This discovery is a major milestone in exoplanet research and astrobiology, as it shows that a rocky planet in the habitable zone can retain an atmosphere, a key ingredient for potential habitability. The helium absorption was detected in 2024 but not in 2025, indicating time-variable atmospheric escape. LHS 1140b has a mass of 5.6 Earths and orbits its M-dwarf star every 24.7 days.

rss · Nature - Latest Research · Jul 17, 00:00

**Background**: LHS 1140b is a super-Earth exoplanet discovered in 2017, located about 40 light-years away. The habitable zone is the region around a star where temperatures could allow liquid water to exist on a planet's surface. Detecting an atmosphere on a rocky exoplanet is challenging; helium serves as a tracer because it absorbs specific wavelengths of light during transits.

## [Sperm Production Restored After 16-Year-Old Frozen Testicular Tissue Transplant](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-02191-3) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A man who had testicular tissue frozen before chemotherapy at age 10 has had his sperm production restored 16 years later after the tissue was transplanted back into his testicles, marking the first successful demonstration of this approach in humans. This breakthrough offers hope for fertility preservation in prepubertal boys undergoing gonadotoxic cancer treatments, who currently have no established option to preserve their fertility. It could transform reproductive medicine for pediatric cancer survivors. The testicular tissue was cryopreserved for 16 years before being autotransplanted; the patient's sperm production resumed, though the quality and quantity of sperm have not been fully detailed. The procedure remains experimental and not widely available.

rss · Nature - Latest Research · Jul 17, 00:00

**Background**: Testicular tissue cryopreservation is an experimental method to preserve fertility in prepubertal boys who cannot yet produce sperm for standard sperm banking. Spermatogonial stem cell transplantation, developed in mice in 1994, involves injecting stem cells into seminiferous tubules to restore spermatogenesis. This case represents the first successful translation of that technique to humans.
