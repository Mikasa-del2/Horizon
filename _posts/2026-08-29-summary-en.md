## [Heisuke Hironaka obituary: smoothing geometry's singularities](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-02688-x) ⭐️ 7.0/10

Nature published an obituary for mathematician Heisuke Hironaka, who died recently. The obituary highlights his landmark 1964 proof of resolution of singularities in algebraic geometry. Hironaka's proof revolutionized algebraic geometry and influenced fields like complex analysis and number theory. His work remains foundational, and his passing marks the loss of a towering figure in mathematics. The obituary notes that his proof addressed singularities such as peaks, edges, and self-crossings. For varieties over fields of characteristic zero, Hironaka's theorem is complete, but the case of positive characteristic remains open for dimensions at least four.

rss · Nature - Latest Research · Aug 28, 00:00

**Background**: In algebraic geometry, a singularity is a point where a geometric object is not smooth, such as a cusp or self-intersection. Resolution of singularities seeks to find a non-singular variety that maps properly and birationally onto the original variety. Hironaka proved this is always possible in characteristic zero, a result that has become a cornerstone of the field.
