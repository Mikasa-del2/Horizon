## [Hacker Wipes Romania's Land Registry Database](https://news.risky.biz/risky-bulletin-hacker-wipes-romanias-entire-land-registry-database/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

A hacker breached Romania's National Agency for Cadastre and Real Estate Advertising (ANCPI) and wiped the entire land registry database after a failed extortion attempt, bringing the country's real estate market to a standstill for a week. This attack on critical infrastructure could have caused catastrophic loss of property records and societal chaos, but an offline backup prevented total disaster, highlighting the importance of air-gapped backups for essential systems. The hacker claimed to have deleted backups, but ANCPI had an offline copy that allowed recovery. The agency is now migrating its applications to Romania's Government Cloud, coordinated by the Special Telecommunications Service (STS), with completion expected by July 22.

hackernews · speckx · Jul 20, 13:28 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48978605)

**Background**: Land registries are critical public records that prove property ownership. In Romania, the cadastre system combines technical and legal data to ensure accurate ownership details. Offline or air-gapped backups are essential for protecting against ransomware and wiper attacks, as they cannot be accessed from the network.

## [EU Considers Sharing Biometric Data with US for Visa-Free Travel](https://edri.org/our-work/the-eu-is-about-to-sell-our-most-sensitive-data-to-the-us-for-visa-free-travel/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

The EU is considering a proposal to share sensitive biometric data, such as fingerprints and facial images, of its citizens with the US as part of a visa-free travel arrangement. This would allow EU citizens to travel to the US without a visa, but at the cost of transferring their biometric data to US authorities. This proposal raises significant privacy concerns, as biometric data is highly sensitive and cannot be easily changed if compromised. If implemented, it could set a precedent for mass data sharing between the EU and US, affecting millions of travelers and potentially eroding privacy protections. Currently, EU citizens can travel to the US visa-free under the Visa Waiver Program (ESTA), but the proposal would require sharing biometric data in advance rather than only at the border. Critics argue that this data could be used for purposes beyond travel, such as surveillance or immigration enforcement.

hackernews · rapnie · Jul 20, 12:14 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48977711)

**Background**: Biometric data includes unique physical characteristics like fingerprints, facial images, and iris scans, which are used for identity verification. The EU has strict data protection laws under GDPR, but sharing data with the US may bypass some of those protections. The US already collects biometric data from visa applicants and at ports of entry.

Moonshot AI, a Chinese startup, released Kimi K3, a large language model with 2.8 trillion parameters that can rival top US models like those from OpenAI and Anthropic. Demonstrations show it can replicate a browser-based macOS in minutes. Kimi K3 suggests the US technological moat in frontier AI is eroding, as Chinese labs catch up within weeks of US releases. This could reshape global AI competition and influence policy and investment decisions. Kimi K3 is an open-source model with 2.8 trillion parameters, designed for software engineering, long-context reasoning, and autonomous agents. It was co-founded by Yang Zhilin, a former CMU graduate student and co-author of XLNet and Transformer-XL.

Chinese AI labs have been rapidly advancing, and Kimi K3 is seen as evidence that the gap is narrowing. Moonshot AI is one of China's 'AI Tigers,' a group of six leading AI startups.
