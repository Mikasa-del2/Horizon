## [Einstein's relativity governs chemical bonds in heavy elements](https://www.brown.edu/news/2026-07-09/chemical-bonds-relativity) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Researchers at Brown University have experimentally confirmed that Einstein's theory of relativity governs chemical bonding in heavy elements, providing direct evidence for relativistic effects such as spin-orbit coupling in sigma and pi bonds. This work validates long-standing theoretical predictions and deepens our understanding of why heavy elements like mercury are liquid at room temperature, with implications for materials science and quantum chemistry. The study, published in Science, experimentally demonstrates that relativistic effects cause spin-orbit coupling, altering electron behavior in chemical bonds of heavy elements. Mercury's inner electrons move at nearly 60% the speed of light, contracting its orbitals and weakening metallic bonds.

**Background**: Relativistic quantum chemistry accounts for corrections to the Schrödinger equation when electrons move at significant fractions of the speed of light. For heavy elements like gold and mercury, these effects influence properties such as color and melting point. While known theoretically, direct experimental confirmation of relativistic effects on chemical bonding has been challenging.

## [Dolly the Sheep's 30-Year Legacy and Ocean Floor Creation Observed](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-02185-1) ⭐️ 6.0/10

Nature staff discuss the 30-year legacy of Dolly the sheep, the first mammal cloned from an adult cell, and the first direct observation of ocean-floor crust creation at a mid-ocean ridge in the Indian Ocean. Dolly's cloning demonstrated that differentiated cells could be reprogrammed, revolutionizing biology and sparking ethical debates. The direct observation of seafloor spreading provides unprecedented insight into how new oceanic crust forms, advancing our understanding of plate tectonics. Dolly was created using somatic cell nuclear transfer (SCNT) and was the only survivor from 277 attempts. The seafloor spreading event was captured using acoustic, geodetic, and pressure-monitoring instruments, revealing 160 million cubic meters of lava creating new ocean floor.

rss · Nature - Latest Research · Jul 10, 00:00

**Background**: Dolly the sheep, born in 1996, was the first mammal cloned from an adult somatic cell, proving that a fully differentiated cell could give rise to a whole organism. Seafloor spreading is the process by which new oceanic crust is formed at mid-ocean ridges, but until now it had never been directly observed in real time.
