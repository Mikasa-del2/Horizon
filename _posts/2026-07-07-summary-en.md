Researchers transplanted cells between comb jelly embryos and observed that organizer cells could instruct host tissue to form a complete body plan, demonstrating functional conservation across evolutionarily distant species.

rss · Nature - Latest Research · Jul 7, 00:00

**Background**: Comb jellies (ctenophores) are gelatinous marine animals considered among the earliest branching animal lineages. Such organizers were previously known in more complex animals like frogs and fish, but their presence in comb jellies suggests an ancient origin.
